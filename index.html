About

I think of myself first as an education and research enthusiast — someone who is as curious about how people learn as I am about teaching them.

I completed my Bachelor of Education (Honours) in Science Education at the Institute of Education and Research, University of Dhaka, and I'm now pursuing my Master of Education in Science, Mathematics & Technology Education at the same institute.

Alongside my studies, I've spent time in real classrooms as a teaching intern, organised student events — from a national science olympiad to an English language carnival — and currently serve as Vice President of my university's bus committee. I'm comfortable managing a room of students, and just as comfortable working through a spreadsheet, a line of SQL, or Microsoft Office to make sense of information. I'm also picking up the practical side of generative AI, and slowly working through German as a third language.
